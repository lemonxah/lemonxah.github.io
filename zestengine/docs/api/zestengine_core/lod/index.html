Screen-size level of detail: pure selection logic shared by the renderer’s mesh swap and the non-geometry distance LOD (animation rate, IK).

“Coverage” is the share of the screen height an object’s bounding sphere covers. Unlike a distance it already accounts for the object’s size, the camera FOV and (through the projection) the window height, so one set of thresholds works for a ring and a building, on a monitor and in a headset.

Constants§

AUTO_LOD_MIN_TRIANGLES
Models with fewer triangles than this get no automatic chain: the draw and memory overhead of extra levels outweighs the saving.
AUTO_LOD_STEPS
The automatic chain every model gets: the share of triangles kept and the minimum screen coverage that level serves.
COVERAGE_HYSTERESIS
Relative margin around a threshold inside which the current level holds.
IK_MIN_COVERAGE
Below this coverage humanoid IK of a non-local avatar is skipped.
IMPOSTOR_COVERAGE
Below this coverage (the last automatic level serves everything under 0.05) a static mesh may draw as an octahedral impostor billboard instead.
IMPOSTOR_PREPARE_COVERAGE
From this coverage down a mesh gets its impostor captured, so the atlas is ready before the switch.

Functions§

animation_interval
How often a far object’s animation is re-evaluated, in frames (1 = every frame). Cheap tiers only: the pose between updates is held, so the steps stay small enough not to read as stutter at that size on screen.
auto_thresholds
Minimum coverages of an automatic chain (descending), for selection.
impostor_distance
Camera distance at which a bounding sphere of radius reaches IMPOSTOR_COVERAGE.
screen_coverage
Fraction of the screen height covered by a bounding sphere of radius at distance, for a camera with tan_half_fov = tan(vertical FOV / 2). An object the camera is inside of covers everything (f32::MAX).
select_by_coverage
Level for coverage out of thresholds (the minimum coverage each level serves, descending; level 0 is the most detailed). previous holds while the coverage stays within COVERAGE_HYSTERESIS of its band so an object does not flicker at a boundary. Empty thresholds give None.
use_impostor
Whether a mesh at coverage draws as its impostor; was (it did last frame) holds it within COVERAGE_HYSTERESIS of the boundary.
