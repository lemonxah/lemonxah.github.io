Expand description

A body of water: an ocean, a lake or a river. Its rest surface is the object’s local XZ plane (a river’s follows its points), the volume extends depth below it. That plane is authoritative: the editor’s basin box hangs from it (its top is the surface), the drawn surface and the simulation rest on it, and ground baked under the water (which may be shallower than depth) only changes how deep the columns are, never where the surface sits.

Density (kg/m^3): 1000 fresh water, 1025 sea. Sets what floats (Archimedes: lift is the weight of the fluid displaced).

§viscosity: f32

Dynamic viscosity (Pa s): 0.001 water, ~0.1 oil, ~10 honey. Sets how thick the fluid is: drag on bodies (how fast they sink or rise to where their density settles them), how fast waves die, how sluggishly it flows.

§fluid_scale: f32

Stage the fluid as a 1:N scale model: it moves like the same fluid N times smaller (a jar of honey in a pool-sized scene). By Froude similarity that is viscosity times N^1.5. 1 = real size.

§drag: f32

Linear drag on submerged bodies (1/s).

§angular_drag: f32

Angular drag on submerged bodies (1/s).

§current: [f32; 3]

Current, in the water’s local space (m/s).

§resolution: u32

Surface mesh quads per side.

§look: WaterLook

How the surface shades.

§quality: WaterQuality§wind_response: f32

How strongly Wind Zones raise waves here (0 = ignore wind).

§interactive: bool

Floating bodies and characters push the surface and leave ripples and wakes (simulated in a window around the player).

§ripple_area: f32

Width of that window (m) and its cells per side.

§ripple_resolution: u32

Cells per side of the interactive ripple window.

§ripple_damping: f32

Share of ripple motion lost per second.

§gpu_sim: bool

Run the interactive layer as a GPU shallow-water simulation (mass, flow, displacement); off, or without a GPU, it falls back to CPU ripples.

§sim_wave_depth: f32

Deepest water the simulated waves travel as (m): deeper water still holds its full mass but its waves move like water this deep.

§sim_displacement: f32

Scales the water a body pushes aside as it enters or moves.

§sim_momentum: f32

How strongly moving bodies drag the water under them along.

§sim_dispersion: f32

Strength of the dispersive wave layer over the simulation (wave trains, Kelvin wakes, chop; 0 = off). Runs weaker over shallow water.

§sim_ripple_mix: f32

How much of the dispersive ripple layer shows (0 = off, 1 = all of it); scales the ripples already there too, not only new ones.

§sim_chop_smoothing: f32

Eddy viscosity on the simulation’s short chop (0 = none, 1 = strong): turbulence drains short waves far faster than long swells, so chop from bodies and impacts dies out instead of bouncing round a pool.

§sim_wake_ripples: f32

How strongly bodies moving through start dispersive waves (their wake); drops and splashes are not scaled by it.

§sim_ripple_smoothing: f32

Seconds a body’s push is spread over: smooths out the frame-by-frame steps that otherwise ring as fine ripples all round it.

§sim_fine_damping: f32

Damping (1/s) of the finest waves the grid holds, so grid-sized ripples die out instead of covering the surface in a checker.

§underwater: UnderwaterSettings

How it looks and sounds from below the surface.

§steam: f32

Steam rising off warm water (0 = none; hot tubs, hot springs).

§project_caustics: bool

Lights under the surface throw caustics out of the water onto the walls and ceiling around it.

§projection_strength: f32

Brightness of that projected light (1 = physical).

§shore_depth: f32

Waves calm where the water is shallower than this (m) over the ground under it (baked from the static geometry): gone at the waterline, full from this depth on. 0 = full waves everywhere.

§runtime: WaterRuntime

Engine-filled wind state, not saved.

§shore: Option<Arc<ShoreDepth>>

The depth of water over the ground (engine-baked), for shore_depth.

§ripples: Option<RippleSim>

CPU ripple state, present while the interactive layer runs (not saved).

§exclusions: Vec<WaterExclusionRegion>

Engine-resolved dry boxes, in this water body’s local space.

§sim_feed: WaterSimFeed

What this frame feeds the GPU simulation (window, displacers, field).

§river_cache: Arc<Mutex<RiverCache>>

The river’s sampled curve, rebuilt when its points change.

§outline_cache: Arc<Mutex<OutlineCache>>

The outline as drawn and tested (outline), rebuilt when it changes.

§fit_request: bool

Asked (from the inspector) to fit the outline and depth to the basin around it; the editor does it and clears this.
